## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a focused, modern, and reliable SaaS productivity environment designed to eliminate cognitive drag and streamline high-volume daily operations. It caters to high-performing product teams, engineering squads, and modern knowledge workers who require maximum clarity, systematic structure, and fluid execution.

The visual style blends crisp modern minimalism with soft tactile refinement:
- **Clean Canvas Architecture:** Crisp slate and pure white foundation layers keep the focal point entirely on user data and workflows.
- **Vibrant Accent Cadence:** Deep royal violet drives focused interactions, complemented by soft lavender atmospheric fills and purposeful status indicators.
- **Quiet Micro-Polish:** Subtly rounded structures, gossamer border dividers, and soft ambient shadows deliver a modern, premium enterprise aesthetic without distracting from content density.

## Colors

The palette leverages a structured slate-to-violet spectrum engineered for sustained readability and high visual hierarchy in data-dense interfaces.

### Core Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#7C3AED`):** The primary royal violet anchor. Applied to primary calls-to-action, active navigation states, primary focus rings, and high-impact key indicators.
- **Secondary (`#8B5CF6`):** Vibrant interactive violet. Used for hover states on primary interactive elements, progress tracking, and key highlighted badges.
- **Tertiary / Base Dark (`#0F172A`):** Deep rich dark navy. Governs primary typographic headings, high-contrast badges, dark-themed utility sidebars, and critical text elements.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`):** Balanced slate grey. Anchors secondary typography, subtle iconography, inactive control rails, and structural borders.

### Surface and Tint Ecosystem
- **Canvas (`#F8FAFC`):** Crisp ultra-light slate background establishing uniform interface contrast.
- **Surface Elevation 0 (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white container surfaces for modular panels, task detail modals, and standard data cards.
- **Surface Elevation 1 / Lavender Tint (`#EDE9FE`):** Soft violet tint dedicated to selection states, subtle highlight strips, interactive chip fills, and badge foundations.

### Functional Status Indicators
- **Success (`#10B981`):** Emerald green for completed tasks, healthy delivery states, and positive metric indicators.
- **Warning (`#F59E0B`):** Warm amber for upcoming deadlines, pending review states, and non-blocking warnings.
- **Destructive / Urgent (`#EF4444`):** Crisp rose red for overdue status tags, critical priority indicators, and destructive workflow confirmations.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the friendly, balanced geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural wayfinding with the neutral, high-density legibility of **Inter** for core functional interfaces.

### Hierarchy & Application Rules
- **Display & Section Titles:** Use **Plus Jakarta Sans** for all structural headings (`headline-*`). Keep tracking neutral to slightly snug (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) on headline-xl and headline-lg to establish strong graphic grounding.
- **Data, Content & Lists:** Use **Inter** across all `body-*` levels. Its tall x-height and clear apertures ensure effortless scanning across multi-column Kanban boards, data grids, and task lists.
- **Labels, Badges & Metadata:** Use **Inter** (`label-*`) with medium-to-semibold weights. For small status tags (`label-sm`), use uppercase styling with subtle tracking expansion (`0.04em`) to ensure legibility on colored chip surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an 8pt base grid unit (`0.5rem`), ensuring consistent optical alignment across nested containers, dashboard widgets, and task cards.

### Grid & Layout Structure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Fluid 12-column workspace layout with `2rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Primary sidebars use a fixed width (`16rem` / 256px), while the central work surface and side panels adapt dynamically to viewport width.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Fluid 8-column layout. Main navigation collapses into an icon rail or off-canvas drawer. Outer margins reduce to `1.5rem` with `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Fluid 4-column layout. Margin shrinks to `1rem` (`margin-sm`), gutters reduce to `1rem` (`gutter-sm`). Horizontal Kanban boards adapt to vertical lists or single-column swipe carousels.

### Component Spacing Rhythms
- Use `space-xs` (4px) strictly for micro-spacing between icons and inline badge labels.
- Use `space-sm` (8px) for input internal vertical padding, icon-to-text separation in buttons, and list item gaps.
- Use `space-md` (16px) for card body padding, stack separations, and form field groupings.
- Use `space-lg` (24px) for dashboard widget padding, table column padding, and section headers.
- Use `space-xl` (32px) to separate major board columns, dashboard cards, and modal dialog sections.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses a combination of soft, ambient tinted drop shadows, delicate low-contrast borders, and clean white-to-slate layering to build depth without visual weight.

### Surface Tiers
- **Base Canvas (`#F8FAFC`):** The ground level of the application. Everything sits on this crisp, low-glare surface.
- **Surface Raised (`#FFFFFF`):** Main board cards, table rows, and dashboard widgets. Outlined by a crisp `1px` border in `#E2E8F0`.
- **Surface Overlay (`#FFFFFF`):** Flyout drawers, context menus, tooltips, and modal sheets.

### Ambient Shadow Hierarchy
- **Level 1 (Subtle Rest):** `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`. Applied to resting task cards and standard content modules.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Active Drag):** `0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.08), 0px 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Applied to cards during hover or active dragging, tinted with subtle primary violet to reinforce focus.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Dialogs):** `0px 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0px 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Applied to central modal dialogs and dropdown menus.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Roundedness Level 2** (`rounded: 0.5rem` / 8px base) to convey a friendly yet disciplined product personality.

### Radius Distribution
- **Controls & Small Components (`rounded`, 8px / 0.5rem):** Applied to buttons, text input fields, badge pills, checkboxes, search controls, and dropdown trigger selectors.
- **Medium Panels & Cards (`rounded-lg`, 12px / 0.75rem - 16px / 1rem):** Applied to task list cards, interactive kanban items, popovers, and table containers. Primary dashboard cards use `16px` (`rounded-lg`), while nested child items use `12px`.
- **Large Modals & App Shell Windows (`rounded-xl`, 24px / 1.5rem):** Applied to central workflow modals, full task inspector drawers, and floating command palettes.
- **Avatars & Status Dots:** Strict circular framing (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for team avatars, status pills, and toggle thumb switches.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#7C3AED` with pure white text, font `label-lg`, height 40px, padding `0 16px`, corner radius `8px` (`rounded`). Hover shifts to `#8B5CF6`. Active state scales subtly (`0.98`) with ambient shadow.
- **Secondary / Soft:** Filled `#EDE9FE` with `#7C3AED` text. Hover shifts to `#DDD6FE`. Provides lower emphasis for auxiliary actions.
- **Outline / Neutral:** `#FFFFFF` background with `1px` solid `#E2E8F0` border and `#0F172A` text. Hover fills `#F8FAFC` and transitions border to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background with `#64748B` text. Hover fills `#F1F5F9` with `#0F172A` text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Height 24px, padding `2px 8px`, corner radius `6px` or fully rounded pill.
  - *Completed:* `#ECFDF5` fill with `#047857` text.
  - *In Progress:* `#EDE9FE` fill with `#6D28D9` text.
  - *Blocked / High Priority:* `#FEF2F2` fill with `#B91C1C` text.
  - *Upcoming:* `#FFFBEB` fill with `#B45309` text.
- **Tag Chips:** Subtle slate fill `#F1F5F9` with `#475569` text, including a leading dot indicator or Lucide category icon.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Base height 40px, corner radius `8px`. Solid `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px` `#E2E8F0` border.
- Text rendered in `body-md` with `#0F172A` color; placeholder rendered in `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state eliminates standard browser outlines and applies a `1px` `#7C3AED` border accompanied by an ambient `0 0 0 3px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.15)` glow ring.

### Cards & Task Tiles
- Resting state: `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px` border `#E2E8F0`, corner radius `12px` to `16px`, ambient level 1 elevation.
- Padding: `16px` (`space-md`) for standard items; `20px` to `24px` (`space-lg`) for primary overview widgets.
- Interactive states: Smooth 150ms ease-out elevation shift to Level 2 with border transitioning to `#DDD6FE`.

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- Checkbox dimensions: 18px x 18px, corner radius `4px`.
- Inactive state: `1.5px` border `#CBD5E1` on `#FFFFFF` fill.
- Checked state: `#7C3AED` fill with an inset crisp white checkmark glyph.
- Radio buttons: 18px diameter circular container with centered 8px solid violet dot when active.

### Lists & Tables
- Table headers: Height 36px, `label-sm` in `#64748B`, uppercase, tracking `0.04em`, resting on a `#F8FAFC` ground.
- Rows: Height 52px, alternating or clean white rows separated by `1px` `#F1F5F9` borders. Hover renders subtle `#F8FAFC` background tint across the full row.

### Specialized Workspace Components
- **Kanban Column Header:** Features a count badge with soft lavender fill `#EDE9FE`, title in `headline-sm`, and trailing quick-add button.
- **Progress Trackers:** 6px track height with `#E2E8F0` background and rounded ends. Active filled track uses a clean horizontal gradient from `#7C3AED` to `#8B5CF6`.
